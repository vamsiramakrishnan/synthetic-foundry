Most synthetic-data systems generate files independently. The files look
plausible alone and fail as a set: a PDF reports a total the workbook cannot
reconcile, a ticket names an employee who does not exist, a board paper cites
information discovered after it was signed.

Worldloom reverses the order:

```text
world state --> events --> canonical facts --> artifact plan --> prose --> files
```

Facts precede prose. Simulation precedes rendering. Evaluation ground truth is
derived from the same state as the evidence.

Four stages, each with a narrow output.

1. **Authoring inputs** say what kind of company this is. A seed, and
   optionally a [specification, pack, facets, locale, or physics
   file](/worldloom/guides/authoring/).
2. **The deterministic world** is everything that is true: the company, its
   people and reporting lines, its systems and services, its events, its
   facts, and its access policies. No model call happens here.
3. **Artifact intents** say which documents that world would have. Each intent
   names a real author, an audience, an access policy, a creation time, a
   knowledge boundary, and the facts the document must carry.
4. **ArtifactIR** is the one resolved structure every renderer reads. Tables,
   sections, claims, and lineage. A renderer decides spelling, never truth.

## The division of labour

You supply judgement and language. Worldloom supplies truth, structure,
lineage, and refusal. The library imports no LLM SDK. Your agent writes prose
through a [checked handshake](/worldloom/guides/narration/): requests out as JSON,
responses back as JSON, every violation returned as data.

## The compiler contract

Compilation refuses an undeclared artifact type, an author who could not have
written the document, an empty audience, a title unrelated to the artifact
family, and content that escapes the declared fact scope. It stamps accepted
IR with a machine-readable `artifact-contract@1` cohesion scope.

This is stronger than prompting a model to keep documents consistent. A prompt
is advisory. The contract is executable, and it is renderer-independent.

## The thin waist

Every subsystem speaks the types in `src/worldloom/models.py`. Generators own
deterministic state. Scenarios append events and facts. Compilers consume
intents. Renderers consume IR. Validators stay independent of the code that
produced the data, so they can [refuse it](/worldloom/concepts/validation/).
