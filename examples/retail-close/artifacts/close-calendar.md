## Commitment

The month-end close completes within **four business days** of period end. Overruns
are escalated to the Group Chief Financial Officer on the day they become known.

## Escalation

Where the committed date cannot be held, the Group Financial Controller notifies the
Group CFO the same day, with a revised date and the reason. The revised date is
recorded against the period.

Workarounds that protect the close date are permitted and must be recorded on the
relevant incident, with any residual risk carried into the following period.
