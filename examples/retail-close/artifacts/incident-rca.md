## Summary

The inventory valuation pipeline failed during the March 2026 month-end close. The
cause was a stale legacy-to-new product hierarchy mapping in Merchandising Hub, which
left 18,412 SKUs without a mapped category node. On-hand stock for those SKUs could
not be valued, so the job produced no output partition for the period.

A manual mapping override completed the valuation the same day. The close was
finalised one business day later than the calendar commitment. There was no P&L
impact on the period.

## Initial assessment and why it was wrong

The first assessment at 09:10 attributed the failure to an overnight Helios ERP
outage. The extract looked incomplete, and an upstream availability problem is the
common cause of that signature.

It was wrong. Helios ERP logs showed no interruption during the valuation window of
02:00–04:00, which ruled the hypothesis out at 11:45. The extract was complete; the
records within it could not be mapped.

This assessment is preserved rather than corrected in place. Any document written
between 09:10 and 13:20 that states the ERP outage cause was accurate at the time of
writing.

## Root cause

**Immediate cause.** The legacy-to-new product hierarchy mapping in Merchandising Hub
(SYS-0002) was stale. 18,412 SKUs had no mapped category node. The valuation job
requires a category node to select a valuation rule, so those SKUs could not be
valued and the job produced no partition rather than a partial one.

**Underlying cause.** The mapping table has no registered owner and no required
reviewer. Merchandising believes the data platform owns it because it is consumed
there; the data platform believes Merchandising owns it because it is master data.
Nobody is accountable for keeping it current, and no approval gate exists to catch
drift.

The mapping has been maintained manually since the August 2024 category restructure,
when the mapping between the legacy and new hierarchy was never migrated into
Merchandising Hub.

**This is a control failure, not a pipeline defect.** The pipeline behaved correctly:
it refused to produce a valuation it could not compute.

## Recurrence

A comparable valuation failure occurred in November 2025 and was traced to the same
mapping table. That remediation addressed the immediate data problem and did not
assign ownership, so the same failure mode remained available.

## Contributing factors

- No pre-close validation of hierarchy mapping completeness. The failure was detected
  only when the valuation job produced no output, which is too late to protect the
  close calendar.
- The four-business-day close commitment creates pressure toward manual workarounds,
  which resolve the period but leave the underlying control untouched.
- The manual override applied on 1 April is not persisted to the master, so the
  master remains stale.
