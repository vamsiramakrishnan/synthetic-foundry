## Position

Group revenue for March finished at 630,000 against a budget of 646,000, 16,000
below plan or 2.48%. Gross profit finished at 157,153 against 166,352, a shortfall of
9,199. Group gross margin was 24.94% against a budget of 25.75%, down 81 basis points.

The shortfall is broad rather than isolated: all three business units finished below
plan on both revenue and margin. General Merchandise accounts for the largest share
of the revenue miss in absolute terms and on a percentage basis.

the immediate problem here.

## Close timetable

The close was finalised on 7 April, one business day later than the 6 April calendar
commitment.

The inventory valuation pipeline failed on the morning of 1 April, leaving 18,412 SKUs
unvaluable. A manual hierarchy mapping override completed the valuation the same day
at 17:30. **There is no P&L impact on the period** — the valuation was completed
before the ledger closed, and the reported result is not affected. The impact is on
the calendar only.

For completeness, the underlying issue is not the pipeline. Review on 3 April
established a control failure: the product hierarchy mapping table has no registered
owner and no required reviewer, so drift is undetected until a downstream job fails.
The same table was implicated in a comparable failure in November 2025.

Two remediation items are open. SCRG-4472 assigns ownership and requires a reviewer,
and is the item that addresses the control failure. SCRG-4471 adds pre-close
validation and addresses detection only.

## Recommendation

1. Note the revenue and margin position, with General Merchandise as the principal
   driver.
2. Require Digital to re-baseline the conversion forecast before the FY27 planning
   round.
3. Treat SCRG-4472 as the closing item for this incident. Resolving SCRG-4471 alone
   would leave the failure mode available for the June close.
4. Refer the ownership gap to Internal Audit for inclusion in the FY26 control
   testing scope.
