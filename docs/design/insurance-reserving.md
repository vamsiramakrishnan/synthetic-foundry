## Decision

The three candidate skeletons were judged independently three times; the reserving vertical won two of the three verdicts and the aggregate score (67, against 65 for the Withheld Rebate and 50 for the Shared Calendar), and the reasoning behind the split is what actually decides it: the one judge who preferred the rebate arc did so on cost-per-hardness grounds while conceding the reserving vertical is "a strong second and the right next arc," and both judges who preferred reserving found it the only proposal that lands entirely on seams banking already proved, prices itself honestly *above* its precedent, and adds a hardness class — supersession without wrongness, authority rank that inverts correctness — that neither existing skeleton can pose at any price. The **Withheld Rebate** (a supplier funding dispute interleaved with the retail close) is deferred, not killed, in one sentence: it deepens the estate's first engine rather than adding a third, and it is committed below as the *next* arc because it is the only design that answers the concurrency half of the review gap — two causally independent processes on one calendar — which this vertical does not. The **Shared Calendar** (domain-registered episode schedules proven by a budget round) is killed in one sentence: it builds the multi-episode generalisation that `domains.py`'s own docstring defers to "a third vertical's real needs," from a second episode on the *first* vertical — the design-the-DSL-before-the-evidence pattern §7 exists to forbid — though its recipe-step-registry slice is correct on its own merits and is absorbed below. This vertical is itself the evidence the deferral was right: three consecutive quarters telling three different stories fit inside `single_episode` with no scheduler at all.

## Premise

A mid-size general insurer runs its quarterly reserving cycle. A 2024 claims-transformation programme quietly accelerated case-reserve setting, distorting the incurred development triangle the reserving actuary's projection factors were calibrated on. At the Q2 valuation the long-tail liability book shows adverse actual-versus-expected emergence; the actuary strengthens the central estimate but can only attribute the deviation as a split — part pattern change (benign), part genuine deterioration (adverse). Finance, under a standing combined-ratio target, books only partial strengthening and releases risk margin to absorb the rest: a recorded, lore-sanctioned decision, the insurance analogue of banking's file-over-challenge. Next quarter the new diagonal proves the deterioration real; the attribution is superseded — routinely, not as the correction of a mistake — the full strengthening is booked, the held-versus-central gap closes, and a retrospective review concludes Q2's decision matched what was knowable then, because the quarterly-only monitoring cadence (lore) made the signal ambiguous.

Why this is a third story and not a third costume. Retail's supersession corrects a wrong hypothesis; banking's restatement corrects a filed error; insurance's supersession is the **normal metabolism of a number whose true value the corpus is structurally incapable of ever containing**. Three mutation disciplines coexist in one episode: triangle observations are append-only and never superseded; estimates form supersession chains in which every superseded link was *correct when made*; as-booked balance-sheet snapshots are frozen forever. And on top sits a permanent two-authority record — the actuarial central estimate (CONFIRMED) and the booked reserve (SYSTEM_OF_RECORD) legitimately disagree forever, reconciled only by an explicit margin fact. It is the first contest in the estate that is never resolved, only explained. That is a question class, not a vocabulary swap — and it unblocks the "ten insurer corpora in ten costumes" complaint at its root, because insurers get native causality plus the whole pack surface (`episode_text`, `evaluation_text`, brands, voices, linted lore) on day one through `register_domain`.

## Causal skeleton

**Coordinates.** Valuation period `P` is the quarter-end month as `YYYY-MM` — the world period, exactly as banking's quarter is, so `period_end`, `previous_periods`, and the boundary arithmetic work untouched and no quarter grammar is parsed. An accident cohort is `(line-of-business category, accident quarter)`, encoded as `fact.subject = LoB category id`, `fact.period = accident quarter YYYY-MM`. That is a deliberate pun on `fact.period`, and it is the design's one load-bearing trick: verified safe today because every core period-keyed check is vocabulary-scoped (`financial.*`, `capital.*`), recorded as a named misfit with its extraction trigger (a Cohort/population axis, which manufacturing lot genealogy would also want — extract at the second consumer, never before), and pinned by a test that no core check ever groups `reserves.*` or `claims.*` by period.

**Standing facts** (minted once, resolved from the world thereafter — the `existing_minimum` pattern `banking_scenarios.py` proved, `world.authoritative(...)` with `period=None`): `reserves.philosophy` (the 75th-percentile margin policy, text) and `reserves.risk_margin_policy_pct` (Quantity).

**The affected book** is resolved by role handle — the archetype (and any pack) must expose `cat_lt_liability`, exactly as banking requires `cat_sme_secured`; the episode refuses a world without one.

**Phase 1** (first run on a world; all timestamps business-day arithmetic after `period_end(P)`, no clock anywhere):

1. Quarter close reusing retail's `close.*` fact kinds verbatim, as `generators/regulatory.py` already does — cross-vertical evaluation code stays shared.
2. **Triangle refresh.** For ~4 accident cohorts of the affected line plus a company roll-up: `claims.paid_to_date` and `claims.incurred_to_date` at `P`, SYSTEM_OF_RECORD, sourced to the claims system. These supersede nothing, ever. The *prior* diagonal is minted in the same run at `valid_from` = the prior valuation date — pure arithmetic into the past, the founding-facts pattern — so the chain exists in a one-period build, mirroring how banking mints filed-then-restated inside one quarter.
3. **Prior estimates**, minted at prior-valuation `valid_from`: `reserves.ultimate` and `reserves.ibnr` per cohort at CONFIRMED (actuarial platform), plus `reserves.booked_total` at SYSTEM_OF_RECORD — the first frozen snapshot.
4. **Emergence.** `claims.actual_vs_expected` per cohort, adverse on the distorted cohorts, CONFIRMED, tagged with the 2024 transformation lore.
5. **Attribution.** `reserves.attribution_pattern_change` and `reserves.attribution_deterioration` — Quantities that must sum to the total movement, a checkable identity — CONFIRMED, lore-tagged.
6. **Strengthening.** New `reserves.ultimate`/`reserves.ibnr` per affected cohort, `supersedes` the priors. The superseded facts carry **no ruled-out marker**, because they were not wrong. This is the discipline the whole vertical exists to exercise.
7. **Committee.** `reserves.committee_recommendation` (book the full central strengthening), CONFIRMED.
8. **The decision.** `reserves.booked_strengthening` (SYSTEM_OF_RECORD, *less than* central), `reserves.margin_released`, `reserves.held_vs_central_gap` — the standing dual record opens. Event actors: CFO and chief actuary; tagged with the actuarial-versus-finance TENSION lore.
9. `reserves.booked_total` at `P`: frozen snapshot. Two validator identities bite here: booked = prior booked + booked strengthening, and booked = central + remaining margin.

**Phase 2** (second `--periods` run; the episode reads the world's own record — an open `held_vs_central_gap` and standing attribution facts — to pick its phase; the `prior_incident_periods` pattern, no new interface):

10. New diagonal appends; actual-versus-expected adverse again on the same cohorts.
11. The Q2 attribution split is **superseded** by a revised split with deterioration dominant — routine supersession of a belief that was the best available when made.
12. Full strengthening; margin restored; `held_vs_central_gap` superseded to nil — the only contest in the estate that closes by *the reconciliation moving*, not by one side winning.
13. `reserves.retrospective_finding`: cites the Q2 margin memo (`derived_from`) and the quarterly-monitoring-cadence lore CONSTRAINT to conclude Q2's decision matched the information then available.
14. Frozen `reserves.booked_total` at `P+3 months`.

**Phase 3+** (any further period, derived from a closed gap on the record): benign steady state — diagonals append, small favourable development, no committee drama — so `--periods N` is defined for all N.

**Temporal cutoffs bite by construction**: a document written at the Q2 valuation cannot see the revised attribution (its `valid_from` is the Q3 valuation date); the retrospective can see everything but must *cite* the superseded pair; and the correct answer to any "as at June" estimate question is a mid-chain link with no lexical marker distinguishing it from its neighbours.

**Five lore commitments, every one load-bearing** (the banking discipline): the 2024 transformation DECISION (why the triangle distorts), the margin-policy NORM (why a release needs a committee), the combined-ratio METRIC NORM (why finance books partial), the actuarial-versus-finance TENSION (why both paper their positions), and the quarterly-cadence CONSTRAINT (why Q2's signal was ambiguous — the fact the retrospective hangs on).

## New mechanisms and seam usage

**Zero core *model* changes; one honest core *edit*.** Every attachment is an existing registration seam, mirroring banking file-for-file: `validate.register_domain_checks("insurance", _checks)` with the same early-return (no `reserves.*` facts, zero cost to stock worlds); `documents.register_artifact_types` for the six types; `render.xlsx/docx/markdown` ownership registration; `archetypes.py` gains `MIDSIZE_GENERAL_INSURER` as data; and `register_domain(Domain(name="insurance", ...))` with `single_episode=QuarterlyReserving`, `period_step_months=3`, `consulted_targets` (`triangle_distortion/long_tail`, `finance/partial_booking`, `reserving_committee_signoff`, `recurrence_after_release`), `system_slots` (policy admin, claims system, actuarial platform, general ledger, reinsurance register), `role_keys` (chief_actuary, reserving_actuary, claims_director, cfo, financial_controller, audit), `unit_role_suffixes=("_md",)`, and the `episode_text`/`evaluation_text` TEXT/EVAL_TEXT tables. Registration at package import, as the seams demand.

The honest edit, which two judges caught the original proposal eliding: a new recipe verb still requires the same two `recipe.py` touches banking made — a `STEPS` entry and a `rebuild` dispatch arm. `QuarterlyReserving` is the **third** such literal, which is the rule-of-three trigger, so this design absorbs the Shared Calendar proposal's one unambiguously good slice: a `recipe.register_step(name, arg_names, build)` registry, seeded at package import exactly like the domain registry (constructors registered in code, never resolved from corpus data — the closed-vocabulary security posture is preserved), with `STEPS` becoming the registry's view and the if/elif chain deleted. It ships as its own byte-gated refactor PR — both example corpora byte-identical through it — and may land before or after increment 1; increment 1 does not wait on it and takes the precedented two-line edit if it lands first.

Genuinely new, honestly stated:

1. **A triangle generator** (`generators/triangles.py`): deterministic cohort emergence — a small factor model drawn from named `Rng` streams, not real chain-ladder — producing paid/incurred diagonals, expected emergence, and the pattern-distortion offset. Nothing existing fits: `capital.py` is single-period single-shot, and retail's `comparative_months` is a P&L trend backfiller welded to retail's CLI path, wrong shape for a cohort-by-development grid.
2. **Phase-aware `single_episode`**: phase resolved purely from facts already on the world. Pattern reuse (`existing_minimum`, `prior_incident_periods`), new only in that consecutive periods tell different stories — and deliberately kept inside `single_episode`, which is what keeps §7a rung 5 and the deferred multi-episode build unjustified.
3. **The triangle XLSX compiler** — new function, existing seam.
4. **The insurance check group**, eight checks: (a) triangle immutability — no `claims.*_to_date` fact is ever superseded or closed; (b) estimate-chain discipline — exactly one unsuperseded `reserves.ultimate`/`ibnr` per (subject, accident-period) per authority stream, each superseding fact dated to a valuation boundary; (c) ultimate = paid + case + IBNR at every valuation, per cohort, within `RECONCILIATION_TOLERANCE`; (d) booked = central + margin at every valuation date; (e) attribution parts sum to the movement they decompose; (f) no strengthening without emergence — a superseding ultimate must be preceded by an A-vs-E fact for its cohort in the same valuation window (banking's correction-before-confirmation, transposed); (g) an unexplained override is a defect — a booked-below-central gap requires a `margin_decision_memo` citing both facts (the audit-access check's manifest-reading pattern); (h) as-booked permanence — `reserves.booked_total` is never superseded or closed (banking's `as_filed_touched`, transposed to a snapshot that recurs every quarter).

Two misfits recorded in the module docstring, banking-style, as §7a strain evidence: **an accident cohort is not an entity** (the `fact.period` pun above, with its extraction trigger), and **the appointed actuary's external peer reviewer is not an entity** — an audience string, the same regulator-is-not-an-entity posture banking recorded.

## Evaluation families and why each is structurally hard

Every case minted through `cases.CaseBuilder` and filtered by `answerable(reachable_fact_ids(...))` — the reachability gate holds by construction, in exact agreement with `unreachable_answer`.

- **temporal_state** — "what was the ultimate for the liability AY-2024 cohort at the June valuation?" walks a chain whose superseded links carry no wrongness marker. Unlike retail's ruled-out hypothesis and banking's restated filing, every document along the chain reads as confident and correct, so recency is actively wrong for any mid-chain cutoff and there is no lexical signal to rescue it.
- **authority_resolution** — central (CONFIRMED) and booked (SYSTEM_OF_RECORD) are both current forever. `AUTHORITY_RANK` picks booked, which is the *wrong* answer for every "what did the actuary estimate" question: rank does not merely fail to resolve, as in banking's tie — it **inverts** correctness for half the family. Resolution requires reading kind semantics plus the margin fact that reconciles the two. And, per the Withheld Rebate graft, each side's figure is quoted verbatim inside the other side's document, so similarity cannot substitute for authority.
- **causal_multi_hop** — why did Q3 strengthen by more than Q3's own emergence? Q2 partial booking → attribution ambiguity → the 2024 transformation lore → the quarterly-cadence constraint: four hops spanning two episode runs, the estate's first cross-period causal chain.
- **numerical_comparison** — booked = central + margin, ultimate = paid + case + IBNR, attribution parts sum to the movement: identities whose terms live in three artifacts at three authority levels.
- **cross_artifact** — the cohort-by-valuation-by-(central/booked) grid mints dozens of near-identical facts differing in exactly one coordinate; an embedding retriever cannot separate AY-2024-at-June from AY-2024-at-September, and the quarterly reports are deliberately near-identical prose. The sameness §7a's diversity work fights is, here, the trap itself — the corpus card must say so.
- **expected_abstention** — "what is the true ultimate cost of the AY-2024 cohort?" is not merely absent but structurally impossible: IBNR is by definition unobserved and the tail never resolves inside the corpus. Hardened, per the Withheld Rebate graft, by confident paraphrase: the committee minutes paraphrase the external peer reviewer's verbal view, so "what does the peer reviewer's own workpaper conclude" forces the system to distinguish the insurer's record of what was said from a fact about the counterparty.
- **citation_required** — the retrospective's mandatory superseded/superseding pair and the margin memo's mandatory dual citation, checked by the validator and asked by the benchmark.

## Pack surface

An insurer pack on this engine may author, on day one and nothing more: the archetype shape (units, lines of business as categories with shares summing to 1 — including the required `cat_lt_liability` handle — site estate, scale); lore in the closed constraint vocabulary, linted against the published `consulted_targets` so inert lore is named, not carried; `system_brands` for the five system slots; `voices` for the published role keys; and `episode_text`/`evaluation_text` overrides of every keyed surface string, published by `worldloom pack texts insurance`. What a pack cannot do is unchanged and restated deliberately: it cannot choose the episode, move a supersession, or touch the physics — a mutual, a Lloyd's-style specialty writer, and a compulsory-lines insurer are the same reserving metabolism in honestly different clothes, which is what a costume *should* be. A reference insurer pack ships in `examples/packs/` as increment 3's proof.

## Cost and risks

**Cost: 1.0–1.3× the banking arc**, priced honestly above its precedent — the only proposal that did. Same file inventory (`insurance.py`, `insurance_scenarios.py`, `insurance_documents.py`, `generators/triangles.py` + `reserving.py` + `insurance_evaluation.py`, an archetype, tests) and the same seam work; three things push above 1.0: the triangle generator has more numeric surface than `capital.py`, the phase logic needs its own determinism tests, and check (b)'s chain-walking is nearer banking's cadence check — its subtlest — than its reconciliations. Nothing requires the diversity or actor workstreams to move first, and no existing corpus's bytes are at risk: this is pure addition, which is why it carried the best determinism scores across all three verdicts.

**Risks, named with their mitigations:**

1. **The `fact.period` pun** is the load-bearing trick. Safe today because core period-keyed checks are vocabulary-scoped; recorded as a misfit with its extraction trigger, and pinned by a test that no core check groups `reserves.*`/`claims.*` by period. If that test ever needs an exception, the Cohort axis extraction is due, not the exception.
2. **Grid blowup.** Cohorts × diagonals × kinds × authorities multiplies facts; bounded by scoping triangles to the affected line, and the generator must derive cohort count from the episode, never the archetype, or a many-unit pack bloats silently.
3. **Diversity tension.** Reserving prose is genuinely repetitive; the similarity trap *wants* near-identical documents, and `worldloom diversity` will correctly flag shape sameness. The plan handshake must vary structure while the numbers grid stays the trap, and the corpus card must explain that tension rather than iterate it away.
4. **Grader fairness.** Chains of unmarked supersession are harsher than anything the baseline has been scored on; expect very low `temporal_state` scores and resist softening the chain — per `designing.md`, that is the corpus working.
5. **Phase-predicate purity.** If phase detection reads anything not rebuilt by the recipe, `--replay` diverges. The predicate reads only the world's own recipe-rebuildable facts; a test rebuilds a two-period corpus from its ledger and diffs byte-for-byte.
6. **Actuarial plausibility without actuarial machinery.** The factor model must be simple enough to stay deterministic and auditable without being laughable to a domain reader: magnitudes inside lore-stated bounds, and a comment saying why real chain-ladder is out of scope — the comment is part of the deliverable, per house style.
7. **The recipe edit.** Whether increment 1 lands the two-line edit or the registry entry, the registry refactor's gate is absolute: both example corpora byte-identical through it, or it does not merge.

The exit gate, stated the way §7 stated banking's: three verticals share the fact ledger, event model, artifact IR, manifest, evaluation schema, renderer interfaces, and provenance system; a third semantics of supersession lives entirely outside core; and the thin-waist ratchet is what makes "lives" true.
